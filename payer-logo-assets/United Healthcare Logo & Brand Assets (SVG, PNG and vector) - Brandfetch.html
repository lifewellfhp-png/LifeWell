About United Healthcare

UnitedHealthcare is a leading brand that offers a wide range of products and services in the healthcare industry. With a focus on Medicaid, they provide comprehensive coverage and support for individuals and families. If you have been asked to renew your Medicaid eligibility, UnitedHealthcare is here to guide you through the process.


They understand the importance of having health insurance and are dedicated to helping you stay covered. Medicaid redetermination, also known as renewal or recertification, ensures that you still qualify for Medicaid in your state. UnitedHealthcare can help you check your Medicaid status and explore other coverage options if needed.


To renew your Medicaid, they recommend three simple steps: check and update your contact information, know your renewal deadline, and complete the required forms. UnitedHealthcare is committed to keeping you informed, protected, and supported throughout your Medicaid journey. Trust UnitedHealthcare, a brand that is there for what matters most - your health and well-being
