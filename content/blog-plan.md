## Editorial guardrails

- Cite reputable education from NIMH, APA, or CDC when discussing symptoms or treatment.
- Explain that an article is general education and does not diagnose or create a clinician-patient relationship.
- Avoid prevalence numbers unless the source and population are clearly stated.
- Do not list medication doses or suggest that one medication is right for everyone.
- Include the 988/911 crisis direction when discussing self-harm, acute safety concerns, bipolar disorder, or PTSD.
